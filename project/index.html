TEAM RAPID JUGGERNAUT RAMPAGE

Who are we?

"We desire to be stronger." Born from a shared drive to push beyond limits, Rapid Juggernaut Rampage (RJR) is an e-sports team dedicated to mastering fighting games, with a primary focus on the Tekken franchise. We persist, improve, and unite—because true victory is built on unlimited passion and relentless perseverance.
