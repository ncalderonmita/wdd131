La Paz Bolivia

La Paz is the vibrant, high-altitude administrative capital of Bolivia, famously perched inside a dramatic, bowl-shaped canyon in the Andes Mountains. Resting at an average elevation of roughly 3,650 meters (nearly 12,000 feet) above sea level, it stands as one of the highest major cities on Earth.
